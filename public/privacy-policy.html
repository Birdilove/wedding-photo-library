Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Wedding Photos Library (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use our wedding photo sharing app at photos.birdistudios.com. Guests don’t need an account: you open the app, take photos, and they are saved to the couple’s private album. We don’t sell your data.

What we collect

  • Photos you take or choose in the app and upload to the wedding album.
  • Technical information such as IP address, browser and device type, which our hosting providers process automatically when you use the app.
  • On your device only: the app remembers which photo prompts you have already seen, using your browser’s local storage. This is not sent to us.

We do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number, and we do not use sign-in, cookies for tracking, or advertising.

How we use it

We use uploaded photos only to collect them in the wedding album for the couple and their family. We use technical information only to deliver the service, keep it secure, and fix problems. We do not sell your information or use your photos for advertising.

Where photos are stored

Uploaded photos are saved to a private Google Drive folder owned by the couple. Photos are not made public, and are accessible only to the folder owner and anyone they choose to share it with.

Services we use

  • Google Drive stores the uploaded photos.
  • Vercel processes the upload request and passes the photo to Google Drive.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the app’s web pages.

These providers handle data under their own privacy policies. We do not share your information with anyone else, except where required by law.

Google user data

The app uses the Google Drive API only to save uploaded photos into the couple’s own Drive folder. It does not ask guests to sign in with Google, and it does not access, read, or store any guest’s Google account or Google data. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

Photos are kept in the Drive folder for [retention period]. The couple may keep the album longer for personal memories.

Your choices

To ask for a photo you uploaded to be removed, or to ask any question about your information, contact us at the address below. Because guests are not identified in the app, please tell us roughly when you uploaded it so we can find it. You can clear the prompt history stored on your device at any time by clearing your browser’s site data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Photos of children taken at the wedding are uploaded by guests for the couple’s album.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will post the new version here and change the date above.

Contact us

Questions or requests? Email us at lovepreet@birdistudios.com.
