Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Wedding Photos Library (“we”, “us”) handles your information when you use our wedding photos app. We keep it simple: your photos are shared for the celebration, and we don’t sell your data.

What we collect

  • Photos and videos you upload to the wedding album.
  • Basic profile details, such as your name and email address or phone number, if you sign in or join an album.
  • Technical information, such as device type and app version, to keep the app running smoothly.

How we use it

We use your information to display and organize the wedding photos, let guests view and download them, and fix problems or improve the app. We do not sell your personal information or use your photos for advertising.

Who can see your photos

Photos uploaded to an album are visible to the couple and to guests who have access to that album. Please only upload photos you are comfortable sharing with these people. We do not share your information with third parties, except service providers who help us host and operate the app, or where required by law.

Storage and retention

Your photos and data are stored securely with our hosting providers. We keep them for as long as the album is active, or [retention period, e.g. 12 months after the wedding], after which they are deleted unless the couple chooses to keep the album.

Your choices

You can delete photos you uploaded at any time. You can also ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information by contacting us at the address below.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will post the new version here and change the date above.

Contact us

Questions or requests? Email us at lovepreet@birdistudios.com.
